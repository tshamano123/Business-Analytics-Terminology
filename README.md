# README – Business Analytics Terminology Exercise

## Overview

This exercise focused on understanding important terms used in Business Analytics. The assignment covered foundational concepts, data and reporting terms, customer and sales KPIs, and marketing analytics concepts. The goal was to research, define, and explain 38 business analytics terms in simple and understandable language.

The exercise helped build knowledge of how businesses use data to make decisions, measure performance, and improve operations.

---

## How the Exercise Was Done

The exercise was completed by researching each business analytics term using credible online and academic sources. Each term was explained in simple English to make it easy to understand. Real-world examples were also included to show how the terms are used in businesses and industries.

The work was organized into four sections:

1. Foundational Concepts of Analytics
2. Data, Measurement & Reporting
3. Customer & Sales KPIs
4. Marketing & Campaign Analytics

Care was taken to explain the differences between similar concepts such as:

* Business Analytics vs Data Analytics
* Metric vs KPI
* CAC vs CLV

---

## Insights Gained

Through this exercise, several important insights were learned:

* Data plays an important role in business decision-making.
* KPIs help businesses measure success and performance.
* Companies use analytics to understand customers and improve profits.
* Marketing analytics helps businesses track campaign performance and customer behavior.
* Dashboards and data visualization make it easier to understand complex information.

The exercise also showed how businesses use predictive and prescriptive analytics to plan for the future and make better decisions.

---

## Summary

In summary, this exercise improved understanding of key Business Analytics terminology and concepts. All 38 terms were researched, explained, and supported with examples. The assignment strengthened knowledge of data analysis, business reporting, customer analytics, and marketing performance measurement.

The exercise also improved research, analytical thinking, and business communication skills.
